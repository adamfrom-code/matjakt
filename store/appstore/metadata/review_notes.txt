Notes for App Review (entered in App Store Connect > App Review Information)

Sign-in: the app works without an account. An account is created in the
app with email + password (no third-party sign-in, hence no Sign in with
Apple). Review account (create one in the app, or use this one):
  Email:    [FILLED IN BY ADAM - review account]
  Password: [FILLED IN BY ADAM]

No preview lock: the app and the server are open. The reviewer can use
the app directly.

Payment: Premium is sold in the app via In-App Purchase (StoreKit 2) as an
auto-renewable subscription. Two products in the same subscription group:
se.matjakt.premium.monthly (1 month) and se.matjakt.premium.yearly
(1 year); switching between them is handled by the App Store. The price
shown in the plan picker and on the purchase button is StoreKit's
localized price for the product - App Store Connect is the source of the
price, and the app hardcodes no amount. The purchase button "Prenumerera"
(Subscribe) is under Konto (Account) > Matjakt Premium and opens Apple's
purchase sheet; the same purchase can be started from the paywall that
appears when a Premium feature is opened. Below the purchase button is
"Återställ köp" (Restore Purchases). The subscription is managed and
cancelled under "Hantera prenumeration" (Manage subscription) on the
account page, which opens the App Store's subscription management sheet.
The iOS build has no other payment path: no card payment, no link to a
purchase outside the app, no code redemption.

Premium is also sold on the web (matjakt.store). An account that bought
Premium there has Premium in the app as soon as it signs in, per
guideline 3.1.3(b): the same subscription is available as In-App Purchase
in the app. A user who already has Premium sees no purchase button, and
the app never refers to purchasing anywhere else.

How to test the purchase: sign in with the review account above (or
create a new account), open Konto > Matjakt Premium, choose Månad (month)
or År (year) and tap Prenumerera. The purchase goes through Apple's
purchase sheet and can be made with a Sandbox Apple ID (Settings > App
Store > Sandbox Account), with no charge. The server accepts sandbox
transactions after full verification of Apple's signature, bundle id and
product id, so Premium is active immediately after the purchase: the
account page shows the subscription (plan and renewal date via the App
Store), and the Premium features - every store's prices, the exact store
comparison, the whole household - are unlocked. Sandbox subscriptions
renew at Apple's accelerated sandbox rate. "Återställ köp" (Restore
Purchases) fetches an existing subscription for the same Apple ID, e.g.
after reinstalling; it requires signing in to the Matjakt account that
made the purchase.

Location: optional, and used only to find stores near the user - either a
postcode the user types in, or, if the user taps "Hitta mig" (Find me),
the device's approximate location (reduced accuracy; the app never asks
for precise location). No tracking, no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Account deletion: Konto > Radera konto (Delete account), directly in the
app.

Prices in the app come from the grocery chains' own data; product
information partly from Dabas. Campaign images and recipe photos are
licensed (Pexels / Wikimedia Commons, credited on the recipe page).
